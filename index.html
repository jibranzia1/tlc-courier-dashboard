    How to read this dashboard
    Date range
    The buttons under the tabs pick the days. Every figure follows them except the late list and the invoice costs, which are always current.
    Handed over
    The day the parcel was packed and booked with the courier in Shopify. Speed is counted from this day.
    On promise
    A parcel on the 9 AM pickup (QWQER, Lahore) delivered the same day; a parcel on the 7 PM pickup (QWQER, PostEx, Leopards) delivered within 24 hours, by 7 PM the next day. Timed from the courier’s pickup scan.
    Average days
    Days from handover to delivery, for parcels that were delivered.
    Settled
    Speed and returns only count parcels handed over at least 5 days before the data ends, so slow parcels have time to arrive or come back.
    Returned
    Parcels that came back to us or are on the way back, as a share of parcels that reached an outcome. From each courier’s own tracking.
    Late now
    Not delivered and past its promise (same day for the 9 AM pickup, 24 hours for the 7 PM pickup). Parcels already on their way back are not counted.
    Busy day
    9 days in 10 have this many orders or fewer. Pickups are planned for it.
    Green and red tags
    The comparison with the same number of days just before the range. Green is better, red is worse.
