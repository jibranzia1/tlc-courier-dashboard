How these numbers are worked out
  • Live figures come from Shopify orders and fulfilments, pulled every morning before 11 AM PKT. Courier names are read from each fulfilment’s tracking company.
  • Orders per pickup leave out cancelled orders. A busy day is the 90th percentile: 9 days in 10 had that many orders or fewer.
  • Next day means delivered within one calendar day of being handed to the courier, using Shopify’s delivered date. Parcels without a delivered date are left out.
  • Costs are the average across all parcel sizes from each courier’s billing, per parcel sent and per parcel that reached the customer. Leopards’ charges exclude fuel, so 10% is added to compare like for like.
  • Return rate = returns ÷ (delivered + returned). PostEx’s is estimated from Shopify voided orders, calibrated on QWQER and Leopards.
  • The cut-off calculator runs in your browser only. Changing it does not change anything for anyone else.
